01 · WHY COVERAGE MATTERS

A child has no salary. The family can still feel a financial shock.

Coverage can create breathing room when the unexpected changes how the family works, cares and plans.

The goal is not to predict the worst. It is to preserve time, choices and financial stability if plans suddenly change.

02 · TWO DIFFERENT JOBS

Death and Critical Illness solve different family problems.

❤️

CRITICAL ILLNESS

A caregiving fund

  • 💼 Replace income while a parent provides care
  • 🩺 Pay for therapy, transport or a helper
  • 🏠 Keep household commitments running

Support during treatment and recovery.

≠
🕊️

DEATH

A family recovery fund

  • 🌿 Meet immediate and final expenses
  • 🕰️ Allow parents time away from work
  • 🤍 Fund counselling and practical support

Space for the family to grieve and reset.
